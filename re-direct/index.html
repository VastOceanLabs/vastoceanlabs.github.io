How it works

For the moment you reach for your phone and you're not sure why.

  1. Tap once and say how you're feeling: tired, distracted, or taking a planned break.
  2. Write a single line about what you're returning to.
  3. Pick a short reset: 1.5, 3, 5 or 10 minutes.
  4. When it ends, re-direct hands you back to the thing you wrote down.

Six resets

Five of them restorative, and one honest one.

Breathe

A 4-4-4-4 box breath with a gentle expanding bubble.

Stretch

Four short body-friendly stretches that loop on longer breaks.

Look away

The 20-20-20 rule: a quiet pulsing dot in the distance.

Quiet

A ring and a single line. Nothing else.

Nature

A soft horizon and a low ambient loop.

Scroll & return

A time-boxed scroll session that ends with an overlay you can't swipe past. Sometimes you just want to scroll, and a bounded window beats an open tab.

What else it does

  • Insights. Tracks the number that matters most: how often you returned to what you wrote down.
  • Reminders, if you want them. They respect quiet hours by day of the week and back off when you ignore them.
  • An app blocker that offers a reset instead of a brick wall. You can always dismiss it and carry on.
  • Nudge someone you care about. Send a parent, partner or friend a re-direct prompt over WhatsApp, Messenger or text. One tap on the link opens re-direct, ready for a break.
  • One-tap start from a Quick Settings tile or a home-screen widget.
  • Accessibility. A reduce-motion switch, a larger-touch-targets switch, and TalkBack support for the countdown and headings.

Local-first, by design

  • No backend, no account, no login, no sync. Every session and setting stays in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • No ads, analytics, telemetry or third-party SDKs that talk to the internet. The app doesn't request internet access at all.
  • Export everything as a CSV any time, or delete it all in one tap.
  • One permission is asked for at first run: notifications. Others are only needed if you turn on the feature that uses them, and the app explains each one first.

Read the privacy policy and the accessibility disclosure for the details.
