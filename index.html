re-direct

Android

When you're tired or distracted, re-direct helps you take a short reset and then get back to the right task. Pick a break (breathe, stretch, look away, a moment of quiet) and it brings you back to what you were working on.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics
  • Everything stays on your phone; the app doesn't request internet access
  • Send a friend a gentle nudge to take a break

How we build

A few rules we hold ourselves to.

Local-first

Your data lives on your device. If an app doesn't need a server, it doesn't get one.

No tracking

No advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs watching what you do.

Calm by design

Fewer notifications, gentler defaults, and nothing built to keep you scrolling.

Plain-language policies

Privacy policies you can actually read, updated before anything changes.
