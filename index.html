How we build

A few rules we hold ourselves to.

Local-first

Your data lives on your device. If an app doesn't need a server, it doesn't get one.

No tracking

No ads, no tracking, and nothing about you sent anywhere: no analytics or third-party SDKs phoning home.

Calm by design

Optional reminders that back off when you ignore them, gentle defaults, and nothing built to keep you scrolling.

Plain-language policies

Privacy policies with a plain-English summary up top, and updated before anything changes.

About

Vast Ocean Labs is a one-person studio. Our first app, re-direct, was built for the small gap between picking up your phone and putting it back down. Every app we make follows the rules above.
